Paper & Ink

A shared journal, kept in two inks.

WarmLiteraryQuietHandwrittenTimeless
Colours
People
AneAbi
Type
Fraunces, Instrument Sans, Noto Serif (widgets), Roboto (widgets), Noto Color Emoji
Icons
Phosphor (Light, Fill for active states)
Build effort
Medium

Calm and personal: the app reads like a notebook you both write in, with a red pencil for anything that needs action. Handwriting fits in naturally. Choose it if you want intimate and timeless rather than flashy; it's quieter than the playful options.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • Handwriting looks native. Doodles, the scratchpad's ruled lines and the pen-drawn tally sit naturally in a notebook language, so nothing feels bolted on.
  • The two-ink person colours (ink blue for Ane, vermilion for Abi) work in every context, from bubbles and photo marks to the map pins and the avatar pair. The couple can tell who did what at a glance.
  • The widget and the app share one look (paper, serif section heads, vermilion actions), and the widget stays within Glance limits: system fonts, solid fills, no blur.
  • It is the calmest and most distinctive board for a private couple's app. The Fraunces numerals, the love note as a folded slip and the alarm screen carry the character with very little decoration.

Trade-offs

  • Fraunces' optical-size and SOFT axes need the variable TTF bundled in Flutter (the google_fonts package only fetches static instances), and Fraunces has no tabular figures. The board flags both as risks.
  • The handwriting engine has to be built three times (Flutter, Compose and a bitmap for the widget). It is the highest build risk, though the board's own plan is sensible.
  • Light paper and warm colours suit daytime best. It is less playful or energetic than Sticker Club or Pebble, and may feel too quiet for people who want a lively, tech-forward look.
  • A serif and paper aesthetic can age well, but a hand-drawn custom map and ruled paper add custom painter work that stock Material would not need.

After Hours

Texting under the covers: lights low, one warm glow each, only the two of you.

IntimateCandlelitHushedTenderUnhurried
Colours

Type
Sora, Manrope, Roboto (widgets), Roboto Mono, Noto Color Emoji, Material Symbols Rounded
Icons
Material Symbols Rounded (weight 300, FILL 1 for active)
Build effort
Medium

Dark, candlelit and private: only the love note, the day counter and the alarm glow. Made for evenings, bed and dim rooms. Choose it if you want romantic and moody; skip it if you want bright, daytime or colourful.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • The two person colours do real work: amber means Ane and rose means Abi on avatars, bubble edges, map pins, gallery marks and assignee chips. You can read who did what at a glance.
  • Glow is rationed: halos appear only on the love note, the days-together ring and the alarm. Everything else is calm tonal layers, which keeps it intimate and OLED-friendly.
  • The widget is buildable. It uses opaque surfaces, Roboto, solid fills, and a quick-action bar that stays fixed outside the scrolling list.
  • The set is complete and consistent. All 13 frames, the full design system and the build notes use the same tokens, and the dark and dawn themes translate rather than just invert.

Trade-offs

  • The love-note and Wrapped gradients and the animated breathing halo take extra custom work in Flutter (radial-gradient stacks, an AnimationController). The widget gets a flat or drawable fallback.
  • Dark-first gives lots of amber and rose on near-black, which is beautiful. The dawn light theme is lovely but visibly secondary, and bright sunlight outdoors favours light themes less well.
  • Sora and Manrope with big radii and pill shapes read as a modern, slightly trendy look. It may feel dated sooner than a neutral Material or editorial style.
  • It suits people who want a moody, romantic app. It suits less those who want a bright, playful, photo-forward or utilitarian feel.

Pebble

Smooth as a river stone, springy under your thumb: Android at its most expressive, made for two.

Type
Roboto Flex (app UI and display, width and weight axes), Roboto (widgets, system font stand-in), Roboto Mono (code and token labels), Noto Color Emoji
Icons
Material Symbols Rounded (weight 400, FILL 0 unselected / FILL 1 selected)
Build effort
Low

The most Android-native option: soft rounded shapes, a rose-and-teal pair for the two of you, big friendly numbers, and widgets that take their colours from your wallpaper. Choose it if you want warm, playful and at home on a Pixel; the widget looks a little plainer than the app.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • Genuinely distinctive for a Material board: each person has a fixed shape (rose cookie for Ane, teal clover for Abi) plus a colour, so 'who' reads at a glance on every screen, widget and wallpaper.
  • Huge variable-width numerals (1,284, 5/8, 12, the 9:00 alarm) and wavy progress lines give each screen one memorable moment while the rest stays calm and tonal.
  • Feels native on a Pixel: the giant widget uses Material You colours from the wallpaper, and the same system carries cleanly to a full dark scheme that is re-picked rather than inverted.
  • The design system and build notes are thorough: every token has a light and dark hex, the motion springs are plotted, all required components and states are drawn, and the contrast ratios are computed.

Trade-offs

  • Flutter fit is good for stock parts, but the cookie, clover and burst shapes, the wavy progress and the spring presses are custom painting work. Roboto Flex's width axis through google_fonts needs checking.
  • In Glance widgets the wide numerals, clipped cookie shapes and wavy lines are not available. Those come from pre-rendered bitmaps and plain bars, so the widget is a simplified version of the app look.
  • Expressive shapes and big type are bold choices that may date as Material trends move on. Some people will find the look too playful or rounded.
  • Suits a warm, playful couple on Android. It suits people who want a quiet, minimal or editorial look less, and it has no iOS feel.

Bento

A tile for every part of your day together.

Colours
People

Type
Bricolage Grotesque, DM Sans, Roboto (widgets only), Noto Color Emoji
Icons
Phosphor Bold (Fill for active/on states)
Build effort
Medium

Everything gets its own coloured tile, so Abi's note, status, water and Goa are all visible at a glance, and the widget matches the app exactly. Choose it if you want a lively, tidy dashboard of your life together; it's denser and louder than the quiet options.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • The giant widget and the dashboard are cut from the same coloured tiles, so the home screen and the app read as one product.
  • Big Bricolage numerals (1,284, 5/8, 12) and one focus tile per screen make the key fact obvious at a glance.
  • Each feature keeps one hue, and the Ane blue and Abi coral marks stay consistent across avatars, chips, bubbles and pins.
  • Dark mode is a real translation: deep-toned tiles, brighter accents, and the overdue tile flipping to bone.

Trade-offs

  • Flutter needs custom tile, grid and rolling-number widgets. Glance can build the tiles as coloured boxes, but it has no letter-spacing, so widget numerals look looser than in the app.
  • The dashboard has about 40 tiles. It needs lazy building to keep scrolling smooth, and the first screen is dense.
  • The coral love-note tile is deliberately very loud. The board lists the dark value and the overdue-tile colour as decisions to confirm.
  • Pastel tiles and rounded blocks suit this moment but may date as the bento trend fades. It suits people who like order, and less so anyone wanting a quiet, text-only look.

Sticker Club

Peel-off playful: the whole app as a sticker sheet for two.

PlayfulChunkyLoud but tidyPeel-and-stickWarm
Colours
People
AneAbi
Type
Archivo Black, Space Grotesk, Roboto (widgets and Android chrome only), Noto Color Emoji
Icons
Tabler Icons (outline, 2 px stroke)
Build effort
Medium–high

Bold outlines, flat bright colours and chunky buttons: the app as your private sticker book. Fun and still readable. Choose it if you love in-jokes and personality; skip it if you want calm and understated.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • The identity is strong and consistent: 2 px ink outlines, flat fills and 4 px hard shadows on every control, plus a die-cut heart sticker, so the app looks like it belongs to a couple and nobody else.
  • The love note, the sunshine scratchpad and the 'today pls' doodle look like things Abi stuck on the screen, and the pinned, unread and overdue states are easy to tell apart.
  • Person colours (cobalt for Ane, tomato for Abi) are used the same way on every screen and always come with An/Ab labels, and the contrast table in the system section shows every text pair at 4.5:1 or better.
  • Widgets are practical to build: a two-layer drawable (ink rectangle plus inset fill and stroke), solid colours and system Roboto, with the quick-action bar fixed below the scrolling list.

Trade-offs

  • Heavy outlines and offset shadows on every row can slow a long Flutter list, so the app needs a custom sticker widget and repaint boundaries. Widgets must use fixed brand colours, not Material You.
  • Loud saturated colour and thick type make dense screens (gallery, long lists) busier than the quieter directions. It needs discipline to avoid looking childish as features are added.
  • The neo-brutalist style is trendy and may date in a few years. It suits playful couples and may feel too noisy for people who want a calm, private journal.
  • Pressed-button, tilt and bounce motion give much of the charm. They must be built carefully and degrade to simple fades with reduced motion.

Aurora

Soft light, just for the two of you.

DreamyAiryLuminousTenderModern
Colours
People
AneAbi
Type
Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Serif (italic), Noto Color Emoji, Roboto (widgets), Noto Serif (widgets), Roboto Mono (board docs)
Icons
Lucide (2 px round stroke, no filled variants)
Build effort
High

Soft pastel light and frosted glass, with an elegant serif for the love note. The most romantic and memorable look, and the widget keeps the feel without blur. Choose it for warmth and a bit of magic; it takes the most care to keep smooth on a phone.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • The love note is the visual centre: serif italic on a coral and lilac glow. It reads as romantic in the app, the widget and the chat, and no other element competes with it.
  • Person colours are used consistently (violet for Ane, coral for Abi) on avatars, chat bubbles, assignee chips, the map and the scratchpad ink, so two people who both start with A are easy to tell apart.
  • The widget is honest about the no-blur rule. It uses a baked gradient bitmap plus translucent white cards, so the home screen matches the app and the Glance recipe is documented.
  • Dark mode is a translation, not an inversion. Indigo aurora, deeper glass, lighter inks and an always-dark alarm all hold up, and the contrast table is measured from the tokens.

Trade-offs

  • Real blur exists only on the dock, headers, sheets and editor card. The rest is flat translucent fill that fakes the look, so Flutter has to be disciplined on a mid-range phone. It needs testing at 90 and 120 Hz, and a Reduce transparency fallback.
  • Text on glass over moving gradients is the main accessibility risk. Contrast passes in the worst case only because the text panels are strengthened (74% white in light, deep indigo in dark). The 55% brief glass can never carry text.
  • Primary actions are midnight ink rather than violet, so the pastel look has a dark note. This is the right contrast call but it is a decision the owners may want to revisit.
  • Glass, gradients and serif italics are very current and could look dated in a few years. They suit people who want a romantic, soft app less than people who want a plain, tool-like one.

Polaroid

A shoebox of the two of you: every screen is a print, a ticket stub or a date stamp.

NostalgicTactileSunlitTenderUnhurried
Colours
People
AneAbi
Type
Young Serif, Karla, Space Mono, Roboto (widgets), Noto Serif (widgets), Roboto Mono (widgets), Noto Color Emoji
Icons
Phosphor Duotone
Build effort
High

Photos are the hero: every picture is a print with a date stamp, the Goa countdown is a ticket, the water goal is a film roll. A keepsake box for the two of you. Choose it for emotion and memories; it's the busiest and most custom to build.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • Photos are the hero. Every image sits in a white print with a date stamp and a who-added dot, so the app reads as a shoebox of memories and not a generic chat app.
  • Distinctive, tidy components: the Goa ticket with real notches, the film-roll goal counter (5/8), the taped love-note print and the dashed perforation rules. Together they make a clear identity without clutter.
  • The home-screen widget stays on brand within Glance limits (system fonts, solid fills, no blur or shadows), and the dark Darkroom theme translates the palette instead of inverting it.
  • Complete and consistent. All 13 frames, the full token and component set, discreet mode and detailed build notes are present, with zero accessibility or QA flags.

Trade-offs

  • Buildability: the ticket notches, film roll, tape, print stacks and grain need custom Flutter painters. In Glance the notches need nine-patch drawables, which is real extra work.
  • Three typefaces plus a duotone icon set and many small decorations. This is the busiest of the directions, so it needs discipline to stay calm as features are added.
  • Small controls (Done and Snooze pills, chips) look about 32 to 36 px tall and rely on padded hit areas to reach 48 dp.
  • The nostalgic cream and film-orange look suits people who like warmth and sentiment. It may feel less crisp or modern to anyone who wants a minimal, utilitarian app, and the film-print style could date over time.

Two

Radical clarity, for exactly two.

PreciseCalmAgelessWarm at the edges
Colours
People
AneAbi
Type
Geist, Geist Mono, Roboto (widgets), Roboto Mono (widgets), Noto Color Emoji
Icons
Material Symbols Sharp, weight 300 (FILL 1 for active states)
Build effort
Low

Black and white with one orange signal and huge numbers: calm, precise and unlikely to date. Your photos and the love note supply the warmth. Choose it for clarity and the easiest exact build; skip it if you want playful or decorated.

Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths

  • The huge numerals (1,284, 5/8, 12) make every screen readable at a glance, and the same numeral language carries across the widget, the app and the alarm.
  • The two-colour rule is easy to learn: black is you and what you do, orange is Abi and what arrives. The love note is the one warm orange block, so it stands out on every screen.
  • The widget is nearly the same drawing as the app: flat fills, hairlines, system font, square inner corners. Jetpack Glance can build it with almost no compromise.
  • The board is complete: all 13 frames, a full design system, real sample data, and no call buttons, rich text or checkboxes where the brief forbids them.

Trade-offs

  • Flat black, white and one orange can feel austere, and it leaves little room for playfulness. It suits people who like order better than people who want cute or decorative.
  • Orange at #FF4F00 is a fill only, with black text on it. Orange text on white would fail contrast, so the build has to follow that rule everywhere.
  • The huge numerals depend on Geist metrics and on tabular, odometer-style animation. In Flutter this needs custom number-rolling work, and very large system font sizes will squeeze the hero numerals.
  • Many checkboxes and small marks are visually 22 to 28 px. They only pass the 48 dp rule if the whole row is the tap target, which the build must keep.
