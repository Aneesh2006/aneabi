08 / Direction

Two

Radical clarity, for exactly two.

  • Precise
  • Calm
  • Ageless
  • Warm at the edges

Two is a strict Swiss grid with two colours for two people. Black is you and everything you do; orange is Abi and everything that arrives from Abi. Numbers carry each screen (1,284 days, 5/8 glasses, 12 days to Goa), and the warmth is left to the three things that should be warm: the love note, the photos you keep, and the orange itself.

How it feelsFast and exact: 150 to 200 ms, one easing, no bounce. Numbers tick like an odometer, a done mark is drawn in one stroke, and a love note lands with a short orange wipe. Haptics are sparing: a light tick on +1, a firm click on Done, nothing on scroll.

Black #0A0A0AYou · text · every action · rules
White #FFFFFFCanvas · space
Orange #FF4F00Abi · arrivals

Greys #F2F2F2, #D9D9D9 and #8A8A8A do the quiet work. Orange is a fill (text on it is always black) and never small text on white. Dark mode inverts the greys and Ane’s colour; Abi stays the same orange on both phones.

01

On the home screen

The widgets are the app for most of the day. They use the phone’s own font, flat black, white and orange, 1 dp hairlines and square inner corners, so Glance can build every pixel; the outer 24 dp radius is the system’s.

02

In the app

Eight screens, one grid: 20 px margins, a 4-column module (84 + 12), an ink rule for every new section, grey hairlines for rows, and one numeral doing the talking. Black is you and what you do; orange is Abi and what arrives.

Home

How the dashboard is organised

  1. Days togetherThe hero numeral. It ticks over at midnight. The bar shows 1,000 → 1,300: ink is days walked, orange is the 16 still to go. Tap opens Us.
  2. Love noteThe one big orange surface on the screen. View, reply and dismiss in place. Your own last note shows its delivered / seen state underneath.
  3. Partner’s cornerA table with two person columns: you on the left (quiet), Abi on the right (ink). Now, mood, place, today. The last row is the unread chat preview and opens Chat.
  4. Mood check-inShown until you answer: five moods, one tap, then it collapses into your column of the table.
  5. Your reminders todayOverdue is the only inverted block, with Done and Snooze in place. The water counter has +1 in place. Done items stay, marked, in a compact pair.
  6. Shared scratchpadEditable in place, live with Abi. Abi’s handwriting is drawn in orange.
  7. NextThe next countdown (Goa trip, 12 days) and the plans of the next 7 days with assignee chips.
  8. On this day · LatestMunnar from three years ago, and what Abi just added. Square crops, a person mark on every tile.
  9. Everything elseWith Chat, Reminders and Gallery on the tab bar, these eight tiles make all 11 destinations visible here: Notes, Calendar & plans, Lists, Wishlists, Countdowns & dates, Map, Wrapped, Settings. Settings also sits in the top bar.
  10. Tab barHome · Chat · Reminders · Gallery · Us. The active tab gets a 2 px ink rule on top and a filled icon. Badges follow the colour rule: orange count = from Abi, ink count = yours to do.

Every item acts in place or opens its screen. The page loads from the local database first, so it opens instantly and offline; then it updates live.

Alarm

The two native screens

  1. Quick-note editor and alarm are ComposeBoth are written in Kotlin so they open in under 500 ms with the app closed. Custom fonts are allowed there, so they use Geist and Geist Mono like the Flutter app.
  2. Scrim, not blurThe quick-note editor dims the home screen with a flat 56% black scrim. On phones where window blur exists, a 20 dp blur may be added underneath; the layout does not change either way.
  3. The alarm rings through silent and Do Not DisturbIt is shown over the lock screen until Done or Snooze. Snooze durations are the PRD’s 5, 10, 30, 60 minutes, with 10 as the default.
  4. Always darkThe alarm ignores the app theme and stays black with white type, so it does not glare at 9 PM. Abi’s orange is the only colour on it.
  5. A “Both” reminder shows both peopleEach person completes it separately. The two squares (and the two cells on the alarm) show who is done.
03

Dark mode

Dark is the system inverted, not just flipped. Ane’s colour is ink, so it turns white; Abi’s orange does not move. The greys invert, the orange wash becomes burnt umber, photos dim by 8%.

04.5

Motion

Precise and fast: 100 to 200 ms, one decelerating curve, no bounce and no overshoot. Motion confirms a change; it never entertains. Numbers tick, marks are drawn, the love note wipes in. With reduced motion on, every animation becomes an instant state change.

Durations--dur-1 … --dur-3
100 mspress, fill start · --dur-1
150 msdigit roll, segment fill, check draw · --dur-2
200 msenter, wipe, sheet slide · --dur-3

Nothing is longer than 200 ms. Stagger by 40 to 60 ms at most.

EasingFlutter · Compose

No springs. Nothing overshoots. Haptics: a light tick on +1, a firm click on Done, a soft double tick when a love note lands, nothing while scrolling.

The orange block wipes in from the left (scaleX 0 to 1, 200 ms), then the text rises 8 px and fades in (150 ms, 60 ms later). The pin ticks once. It stays until dismissed.

The old digit leaves upward and the new one rises from below (150 ms) while the next segment fills left to right (120 ms). The widget does the same with a crossfade, since Glance cannot animate.

The square fills ink (100 ms), the tick is drawn in one stroke (150 ms), then a 1 px strike runs through the title (150 ms) and the text drops to --mute. Undo reverses it.

05

Notes for the build

What to take from the framework, what to build once and reuse, what could go wrong, how the widgets keep this look inside Glance’s limits, and what accessibility needs from the build.

05.1Flutter: stock and custom

Stock, themed
  • Theme from the tokensA ThemeData whose ColorScheme maps bg, ink and signal, plus one ThemeExtension that carries the rest: person colours, line, faint, mute, wash, ok, danger and the type styles. Light and dark are two instances of it.
  • Scrolling and layoutScaffold, SafeArea, CustomScrollView with slivers (the dashboard is one sliver list), ListView.builder, SliverGrid for the gallery.
  • Inputs and overlaysTextField with a one-line InputDecoration (1 px ink border, radius 2), showModalBottomSheet for the map sheet and love-note reply, a floating SnackBar with an Undo action.
  • Fonts and iconsBundle Geist and Geist Mono as assets (offline first; do not fetch at runtime) and drive weight with FontVariation. material_symbols_icons for Material Symbols Sharp, weight 300, subset to the 66 icons listed.
Custom, build once
  • NumeralsHeroNumeral (FittedBox, tight tracking, orange comma via TextSpan) and RollingNumeral (AnimatedSwitcher, 150 ms slide).
  • Meters and marksSegmentMeter (a Row of equal boxes, outlined “next” segment), PersonMark, PairMark, AssigneeChip with the split-square “Both”, the two-square “Both” state.
  • StructureHairlineSection (ink rule, label, meta), TwoColumnTable (a Table with a time or label column, then you, then Abi), InvertedBand, TabBar with a 2 px ink rule (not the stock NavigationBar).
  • ContentLoveNoteCard, VoiceWaveform, MessageBubble (square tail corner), HandwritingCanvas, SquareSwitch, PhotoTile with person mark, StickerSvg through flutter_svg, the drawn map.

05.2Risky or fiddly

  • The mapThe drawn map is a stylised stand-in. The real one needs a monochrome style: white roads on #EDEDED, hatched water and parks (sprite patterns), no labels except saved places. Plan a MapLibre style file as a deliverable; the fallback is raster tiles under a greyscale ColorFilter with the same pins. Decide when M7 starts.
  • Contrast of orangeThe easiest rule to break. White on orange is 3.3:1 and fails; orange text on white fails below 24 px. Add a golden or lint check that no TextStyle on a signal fill uses anything but --on-signal.
  • Text on photosThe Goa card, On this day and tile overlays rely on a baked gradient scrim. Tooling cannot measure contrast over an image, so check by eye on bright and dark photos.
  • Giant numerals under large textDisplay styles are already huge. Clamp their text scale to 1.15 and use FittedBox so “1,284” never clips; everything else follows the system scale up to 200%.
  • Live cursorsThe flag follows the CRDT position with an 80 ms ease. Handle the flag colliding with the first line and with a wrapped line end.
  • Handwriting parityStrokes are stored as points with an ink token. The widget bitmap is drawn natively in Kotlin from the same points with the same smoothing, or the scratchpad will look different on the home screen than in the app.
  • Alarm over the lock screensetShowWhenLocked, setTurnScreenOn and the full-screen-intent permission, which Android 14 and later can revoke. The phone-health row must show it red the moment it is off.

05.3Widgets inside Glance’s limits

  • ColourFixed brand colours, not Material You. Each token becomes a ColorProvider(day, night). The widget never reads wallpaper colours, so Abi is the same orange on both phones.
  • TypeFontFamily.SansSerif for text, FontFamily.Monospace for labels, weights Normal, Medium and Bold only, no letter-spacing. Labels are upper-cased in the string. Hero numerals are Normal at 34 to 84 sp.
  • ShapesThe outer 24 dp radius comes from the system (@android:id/background with appWidgetRadius). Inner blocks use GlanceModifier.cornerRadius(0 to 4 dp). Hairlines are 1 dp Boxes. Segment meters are a Row of Boxes with defaultWeight().
  • StructureThe giant widget is a LazyColumn of four sections with the quick-action bar in a Row outside the list, so it never scrolls. Small widgets are fixed Columns and Rows; sizes use SizeMode.Responsive for 2×1, 2×2, 2×3 and 4×3.
  • ImagesThe Countdown photo (scrim baked in) and the scratchpad handwriting are bitmaps produced in a WorkManager job at 2× size. All text on top of them is live Glance text.
  • No effectsNo blur, no glass, no shadow, no dot-grid background (the dots on the app’s scratchpad are drawn into the handwriting bitmap only). Discreet mode swaps the content composables for name-and-count rows.
  • ActionsEvery tap is a 48 × 48 dp clickable Box that updates local state first and the widget in the same frame (done, +1, tick), then syncs. Data older than 15 minutes adds “updated 7:02 PM” to the section label.

05.4Accessibility

  • ContrastAll text meets 4.5:1 in both themes: ink 19.8, mute 5.3 on bg and 4.8 on surface and 4.7 on the orange wash, black on orange 6.0; in dark, mute is 7.9 and orange on black is 6.0. Numerals of 24 px and up may use the 3.3:1 orange or --faint.
  • Touch targetsEvery control is 48 × 48 dp. The 28 dp check, 24 dp chip and 40 dp small button carry a 48 dp hit area (MinimumInteractiveComponentSize or InkResponse). Nothing is smaller than 12 px type.
  • Never colour alonePerson is colour plus two letters plus side of the screen. Done is a check, a strike and a calmer colour. Overdue is inversion plus the words “Overdue 1 h 15 min”. Phone health is an icon plus a sentence.
  • Text sizeThe layout survives 200% font scale: rows grow, labels wrap, the “you / Abi” table stacks you above Abi from 1.5×, and widget text scrolls instead of clipping.
  • Screen readersNumerals read as sentences (“Water, 5 of 8 glasses, add one”), icon buttons are labelled, decorative marks are hidden, and the love note is announced as “Love note from Abi, 5:58 PM, pinned”.
  • Motion and hapticsRolls, draws and wipes become instant changes under “remove animations”. Haptics follow the system setting and are tied to the same events (+1, Done, love note).
  • FocusA 2 px ink ring with a 2 px offset (white in dark) on every focusable control. It is never removed.
