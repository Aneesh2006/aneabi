The idea

Ane & Abi’s space is lit like the sky just before sunrise: peach, lilac, mint and sky drift slowly behind frosted panels, so everything feels held rather than stacked. Colour belongs to the two of you, violet for Ane and coral for Abi, and the rest of the interface stays quiet, midnight ink on glass, so photos, love notes and the small serif italic moments are what you notice.

It suits a private app for two because it never shouts. It glows.

How it feels

Motion. The aurora drifts a few pixels a minute and panels float on a gentle 8 px parallax. Counters fill like light (sky to mint, 400 ms), marking done draws a tick and the row softens, and a love note arrives with its coral glow breathing three times.

Haptics. Light and rare: a tick for done, a soft double tick like a heartbeat for a love note, nothing while scrolling.

Everything can be acted on

Reply and Dismiss the love note, tap a mood, Snooze, Done, +1 and tick reminders, type or draw on the scratchpad, open the chat from the preview, open a memory. Cards that cannot be changed in place open their screen on tap.

A native screen, in the night palette

  • Built in Compose, shown over the lock screen with the alarm sound and vibration, ringing until dismissed or snoozed. It rings through silent mode and Do Not Disturb.
  • Always dark: a 9 PM alarm should not glare. The aurora is a static pre-rendered gradient here; the rings pulse.
  • Done is one huge target. Snooze offers 5, 10, 30 and 60 minutes, with 10 as the default.
  • For a reminder shared with Abi it shows “Abi’s done ✓” and your own streak. “Tell Abi” fires on done or missed, as set on the reminder.
  • Custom fonts are fine here, so Plus Jakarta Sans is used at weight 300 for the clock.

04 · The kit

Design system

Everything the build needs, in the order you will reach for it: colour, type, shape, icons, motion, components and the widget kit. Token names here are the CSS custom properties on :root (light) and [data-theme="dark"] in this file, so they can be copied straight into Flutter’s theme.

Colour

Colour belongs to the two of you. Violet is Ane and coral is Abi, everywhere: avatar, chip, bubble, pin, pen and cursor. The aurora is the atmosphere, action is a quiet midnight ink, and red appears only for overdue or destructive. Every value below is the live token; swatches sit on checkerboard so the glass levels are visible.

Aurora drift and parallax

Blobs translate about 34 px over 48 s (alternate). Panels counter-float ±8 px at 0.3× the scroll speed, so glass seems to hover over the light. In Flutter the drift is a single AnimationController on a RepaintBoundary that holds only the background; the content never repaints because of it.

Widget kit

Widgets cannot blur, so the aurora is baked. The app pre-renders one soft gradient bitmap per size and theme and lays translucent white cards on top: the same frosted look, buildable in Jetpack Glance. Widgets use fixed brand colours (not Material You), with a light and a dark surface that follow the system theme, and the phone’s own fonts (Roboto, Noto Serif).

How the glass is faked

Three layers, no blur, no shadow. Everything is allowed in Glance: an image background, drawables with a solid translucent colour, a 1 dp stroke and a corner radius, then text and vector icons.

Glance recipe

Render the bitmap at a third of the widget size; a gradient has no detail, so it scales up smoothly and stays well inside the RemoteViews bitmap budget.

// res/drawable/glass_card.xml  (dark: #1AFFFFFF fill, #24FFFFFF stroke)
<shape android:shape="rectangle">
  <solid android:color="#C7FFFFFF"/>       <!-- 78% white -->
  <stroke android:width="1dp" android:color="#EBFFFFFF"/>
  <corners android:radius="22dp"/>
</shape>

// Kotlin (Glance)
Box(GlanceModifier.fillMaxSize().background(ImageProvider(auroraBitmap))) {
  Column(GlanceModifier.fillMaxSize()) {
    LazyColumn(GlanceModifier.defaultWeight()) { /* love, partner, reminders, pad */ }
    QuickActionBar()   // fixed, outside the scrolling list
  }
}

Notes for the build

What is stock, what is custom, what is risky, how the widgets stay inside Glance, and what to test for accessibility. Written so the winning direction can be built exactly as drawn.

Stock in Flutter

Use the framework as is, themed from the tokens.

  • Structure: Scaffold, CustomScrollView with slivers (the dashboard), ListView.builder (chat, gallery), SafeArea, showModalBottomSheet for the map sheet and pickers.
  • Inputs: TextField with one shared InputDecorationTheme (pill, glass fill, 2 px accent focus ring), Switch with custom track colours, SnackBar restyled as the midnight pill with an Undo action.
  • Text and icons: google_fonts (or the same TTFs bundled as assets), lucide_icons_flutter, Semantics everywhere.
  • Theme: one ThemeExtension per token family (AuroraColors, AuroraType, AuroraShape), light and dark built from the CSS tokens in this file.

Custom

Small, reusable pieces that carry the look.

  • AuroraBackground: a CustomPainter with four radial blobs, inside one RepaintBoundary, driven by a single 48 s AnimationController.
  • GlassCard: two modes. Flat (fill + 1 px edge + inner highlight, no blur) for everything in a list; Blur (BackdropFilter, sigma 20) for the dock, headers, sheets and the editor card only.
  • GradientEdge (hero hairline), GoalBar (eight segments), PersonAvatar and AssigneeChip, LoveNoteCard, message and voice bubbles with a waveform painter, FloatingDock.
  • HandwritingCanvas: pointer events into a smoothed path, strokes cached per block, the same strokes rendered to PNG for the widgets.
  • Map: a MapLibre style that reuses the --map-* tokens (land, block, road, park, water), with pins and the accuracy ring as widget layers.

How the widgets keep the look

  • Fixed brand colours, not Material You. Light and dark follow the system theme.
  • Surface: pre-rendered aurora bitmap. Cards: translucent white drawables with a 1 dp stroke and 22 dp corners. Text: Roboto, with Noto Serif italic for the love note. Icons: Lucide as vector drawables.
  • The giant widget is one scrolling list; the quick-action bar sits below it and never scrolls. Done items stay visible. Small widgets reuse the same card drawables.
  • Touch targets are 48 dp (36–40 dp visuals with a larger clickable area); taps update the widget immediately and sync after.
  • Discreet mode swaps each card for a name and a count.

Accessibility

  • Contrast: body text 4.5:1 and large text 3:1 on every surface, including worst-case glass. Person colours are never the only cue: names, positions and icons always accompany them. Overdue is red plus an icon plus the word.
  • Touch: every control 48 dp or more; chips and rows have hit slop. Nothing important is under 12 px, body is 15.
  • Large text: layouts use min heights, wrap onto a second line and scroll. At 130% and above the dock grows, rows become two lines and the widgets show fewer rows.
  • Screen readers: every icon button is labelled; the avatar pair reads “Ane and Abi”; the love note is first in focus order; handwriting blocks announce as “Handwriting”; the map offers a list of places as an alternative.
  • Motion and haptics: reduce-motion stops the drift and the glow; haptics can be turned off. Copy never uses gendered pronouns.
  • Transparency: “Reduce transparency” and high-contrast modes replace glass with solid surfaces and darker borders.

Decisions the owners may want to revisit

  • Action colour is midnight ink, not violet. It keeps violet and coral meaning only Ane and Abi. The cost is a darker note in a pastel world; it gives the strongest contrast for primary actions.
  • The dock holds Home, Chat, Gallery, Reminders and Together. Notes, Calendar, Lists, Wishlists, Countdowns, Map, Wrapped and Settings are one tap away in “More of your space” on Home. Notes could swap places with Reminders if you write more than you tick.
  • Chat is a full-screen conversation that hides the dock, like an Instagram DM. The mic and Send sit side by side in the composer, so a draft never hides the voice note.
  • The alarm is always dark. At 9 PM a bright lock-screen alarm is harsh, so it ignores the system theme.
