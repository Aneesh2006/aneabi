Direction 07 of 08

Polaroid

A shoebox of the two of you: every screen is a print, a ticket stub or a date stamp.

  • Nostalgic
  • Tactile
  • Sunlit
  • Tender
  • Unhurried

This app holds exactly two people, so it behaves like the shoebox under the bed. Photos sit in white polaroid frames, countdowns are perforated tickets, and every moment carries a small orange film date stamp. The chrome stays quiet cream and ink so the memories are always the loudest thing on screen, and “our story” is the thread from the home-screen widget to the alarm.

How it feelsPrints drop onto the table and settle with one small wobble (220 ms, a single overshoot). +1 on a counter develops the next frame of a film roll with a light haptic tick. Marking something done presses a date stamp into the row. The Goa ticket punches a hole on the day. Nothing bounces twice and nothing is ever slower than your thumb.

01 · Home screen

On the home screen

The widget is the part of the app you see most. It is a cream card with white prints on it, system fonts only, solid fills and no blur, so Glance can build every pixel of it. Brand colours are fixed (not Material You) because the film-print palette is the identity.

03 · Dark mode

Darkroom

Dark mode is translated, not inverted. The table goes dark and the UI cards go warm brown, but prints stay paper: the love note, every photo frame and the mood prints glow against the table like fresh prints under a safelight. Person colours stay exactly the same; only their text tints lighten.

What translates, what stays

  1. Table and cardsCream #F3EBDD becomes darkroom #15110E; white cards become #211A15.
  2. Prints stay paper#F1E8D8 in dark (a touch dimmer than daylight to avoid glare), always with brown text.
  3. PeopleAne #3F7F7A and Abi #C2553D never change. Text tints lighten to #86CBC3 and #F09C88.
  4. ActionKodak yellow #F6C94C is the same in both, and gains contrast on dark (12:1).
  5. Shadows go quietOn dark, depth comes from lighter surfaces and hairlines; only prints keep a soft black shadow.
  6. Widgets and alarmThe widget follows the system theme with the same tokens. The alarm is always dark.

04 · Design system

Design system

Everything below is one set of tokens. The names are the CSS custom properties on :root of this file (Daylight) and on [data-theme="dark"] (Darkroom), so the Flutter theme, the Glance resources and the Compose editor can copy them one to one.

05 · Notes for the build

Notes for the build

What Flutter gives for free, what is ours to draw, where the risk is, how the widget stays inside Glance, and the accessibility rules this direction commits to.

Stock vs custom in Flutter

AreaStock widget, themed from the tokensCustom, drawn by us
ThemeThemeData for Daylight and Darkroom built from the colour tokens; a ThemeExtension carries print, film, ane, abi and the elevation tokens.Nothing. Tokens are the single source; no hard-coded hex in widgets.
TypeTextTheme from the type table; Young Serif, Karla and Space Mono via google_fonts (or bundled). One weight of Young Serif: never synthesise bold.StampText: Space Mono 12 caps with 0.08 em tracking and tabular figures.
NavigationNavigationBar (5 destinations, 84 dp, yellow indicator), Scaffold, route transitions.The perforated top edge of the bar (a dashed CustomPaint).
InputsTextField with InputDecoration, Switch, SegmentedButton (ink selected), SnackBar with action, modal bottom sheets, dialogs.Quick-add preview card; mood picker (five mini prints).
ListsCustomScrollView with SliverList and SliverGrid (gallery, 4 columns), Dismissible / swipe-to-reply.Rows separated by PerfDivider instead of Divider.
PhotosImage from the encrypted cache, InteractiveViewer for zoom, video player.PrintFrame (white frame, thicker bottom, caption, stamp, who-added dot, favourite heart); stacks with ±2° rotation; the film tint as a ColorFilter.matrix on thumbnails only.
Tickets and prints—Ticket (a CustomClipper path with half-circle notches plus a dashed divider painter), Tape (clip path with torn ends), LoveNoteCard (a print with a tinted window).
Counters—FilmRoll (CustomPainter, N frames, M exposed, next frame outlined) with the develop animation; GoalCounter wraps it with the +1 button.
Handwriting—InkCanvas: strokes stored as points with width and ink, drawn as round-cap paths; the same stroke data is rendered to a transparent PNG for widgets and the Kotlin editor.
Voice and chatListView.builder reversed for the thread.WaveformBar painter, bubble shapes, the sticker renderer (SVG assets).
MapMapLibre (or equivalent) for the base map.A custom map style that uses the map colour tokens; pins, accuracy circle and saved-place chips are Flutter widgets above the map.
Texture—GrainTable: one 128 px tileable noise PNG at 4–6% opacity on the table layer only, inside a RepaintBoundary.

Risky to build

  • HighHandwriting across three renderers. Dart, the Kotlin quick-note editor and the widget bitmap must agree on the stroke format and the pen widths. Test round-tripping on both phones before anything else.
  • HighTicket notches in Glance. Needs a nine-patch or vector drawable per size class with a fixed notch offset from the bottom. Verify on the Pixel and the OnePlus launchers; the fallback is a plain rounded card with a dashed divider.
  • MediumFilm tint and grain cost. Tint cached thumbnails, never originals or the full-screen viewer; make tint and grain a setting. Skip grain under battery saver.
  • MediumRotated print stacks in scrolling lists. Keep stacks to four prints, wrap each in a RepaintBoundary, never rotate the gallery grid.
  • MediumDuotone icon colours. The back layer needs its own colour (Kodak yellow) in a few places; wrap the package icon in one helper.
  • LowEmoji. Use the phone’s emoji font. Do not bundle Noto Color Emoji (this board loads it only to mimic Android).
  • LowHaptics timing. Light tick on +1, click on done, soft double tick when a love note lands; all off with reduce motion.

How the widget stays within Glance

  • Colours: fixed brand colours from colors.xml with a values-night set. No Material You. Day or night follows the system theme.
  • Fonts: system only: FontFamily.SansSerif (Roboto), Serif (Noto Serif) for headers, Monospace (Roboto Mono) for stamps and counters. Verify the Glance FontFamily options at build time.
  • Shape: shell 28 dp, cards 16 dp, buttons 16 dp, all as drawables; a 1 dp stroke replaces every shadow.
  • Prints: a white Box with padding (larger at the bottom) around an Image. No rotation and no tape; those exist only in the app.
  • Film roll: a Row of 8 equal Boxes; the first M are filled with accent.
  • Handwriting: the scratchpad is text, then an Image of the ink, then text. It is its own block, so there is no overlay alignment to get wrong.
  • Layout: one LazyColumn of sections; the quick-action bar is a fixed Row under it. Every button is an actionRunCallback with an optimistic update.
  • Limits respected: no blur, no glass, no arbitrary shadows, no animation, text ≥ 12 sp, actionable rows ≥ 48 dp.

Accessibility

  • Contrast: ink 13.3:1, muted ink 5.5:1, film-ink 5.0:1, ink on Kodak yellow 10:1, white on Ane 4.6:1, on Abi 4.5:1, danger 5.4:1. Nothing decorative carries text.
  • Targets: every control has a 48 × 48 dp hit area; chips are 28–36 dp tall visually and padded. +1 is 48 dp or larger.
  • Never colour alone: person colours always come with An / Ab monograms or a name; overdue always has a word and an icon; green and red rows have text.
  • Bigger text: body is 16 sp and everything wraps. At 200% the bottom bar keeps icons and shows only the selected label; prints keep their size and captions wrap under them.
  • Screen readers: each print reads its caption or date and who added it; the film roll reads “5 of 8 glasses”; the Goa ticket reads “Goa trip, 12 days, Saturday 17 October”; stamps are real text, never baked into photos.
  • Motion: with reduce motion on, wobbles, the develop effect and the stamp become plain fades. Nothing flashes.
  • Darkroom: prints are dimmed to #F1E8D8 so they do not glare on OLED; the film tint can be turned off in Settings.

Choices this direction adds

  • Film look (Settings › Gallery): tints thumbnails warm and slightly faded. On by default; never touches originals or the full-screen viewer.
  • Paper grain: on by default, off under battery saver or reduce motion.
  • Print tilt: the ±2° on photo stacks is static decoration; it stays on with reduce motion because nothing moves.
  • Counter widget: shows one goal reminder (Water by default) and its +1; the owner can pick another goal.
  • Love-note tape: the strip is drawn in the app and the chat only. The widget print has none, since Glance cannot overlap shapes freely.
  • Wallpaper blur behind the quick-note editor and the alarm is an enhancement where the phone supports it; the scrim is the design.
