Concept

A private app for two should feel like something you hold, not something you operate. Pebble takes Material 3 Expressive at face value: tonal colour grown from one raspberry seed, huge variable-width numerals, and shapes that morph under your thumb.

Each of you gets a shape of your own, a rose cookie for Ane and a teal clover for Abi, so “who” is never just a colour. Everything else stays calm and native, so it sits at home on a Pixel and builds from stock Material parts.

How it feels

Springs, not curves. A press squishes a shape and releases it with a small overshoot (spatial springs); colour and opacity settle without bounce (effects springs). Done snaps with a confirm tick, +1 gives a soft segment tick, and a love note lands with a gentle double pulse and a burst seal that unfolds.

With reduced motion on, springs become 100 ms fades and haptics stay.

Colour

Every colour is a tonal role grown from one seed. Names match the CSS custom properties on this board, so the build can copy them one for one. The dark scheme comes from the same seed, so every role has a light and a dark value.

#B33B5E

Seed raspberry-rose #B33B5E, expressive variant. Primary and secondary come from the rose hue, tertiary is the complementary teal. Success, flame and sticky are extended roles tuned to the same tonal steps so they sit with the rest. Ane is the primary family, Abi the tertiary family, everywhere: avatar, chip, bubble, cursor, pin. A person’s colour is fixed on both phones.

Motion

Springs, not curves. Spatial springs move and morph things and are allowed to overshoot. Effects springs change colour and opacity and never overshoot. These are the Material 3 Expressive default tokens, drawn from their real curves.

Spatial · fast--spring-spatial-fast

Small things that move or change shape: buttons, switches, check cookies, +1.

Spatial · default--spring-spatial-default

416ms

Containers and cards: love note arriving, sheets, list reorder.

572ms

Full-screen moves: alarm in, Wrapped, hero numerals.

Notes for the build

What is stock and what is custom in Flutter, what is risky, how the widgets keep the look inside Glance, and the accessibility checks behind the numbers on this board.

Stock vs custom in Flutter

PartStock MaterialCustom
ThemeColorScheme.fromSeed, seed #B33B5EExtended roles (success, flame, sticky) and person colours as a ThemeExtension
Nav bar, FAB, chips, switch, text field, snackbar, sheetsStock M3 widgets, restyled only through theme and shape tokensNothing
Connected button groupSegmented button as the baseSelected-to-pill morph, per-person selected colour
Expressive shapesNone in stockCookie, clover, burst and pebble as CustomClipper<Path> from the normalised clip paths on this board
Wavy progress, spring pressSpring tokens exist as numbersCustomPainter for the wave, SpringSimulation for presses and morphs. Check whether the Flutter release in use ships wavy indicators
Typegoogle_fonts Roboto FlexWidth and weight axes through FontVariation. Needs a check that the package exposes wdth; if not, bundle the Google Fonts file as an asset
Map, handwriting, stickersNoneSVG paths drawn on a canvas; handwriting as stored strokes, rendered to a bitmap for widgets

Risky to build

  • Variable-width type in widgets. Not available. Widgets get Roboto at plain weights; the huge wide numerals are an in-app moment only. Widget numerals use bold Roboto.
  • Shapes in widgets. Glance can’t clip to a cookie. The cookie and clover marks are pre-rendered vector drawables or bitmaps.
  • Handwriting in the widget scratchpad. Render the strokes to a transparent bitmap in the person’s ink colour. It must be redrawn when the colour scheme changes.
  • Spring motion at 120 Hz on long lists. Keep springs on small items and let lists scroll natively.
  • Live note cursors. Presence has to be debounced so the cursor doesn’t jitter.
  • Window blur behind the quick-note editor. Only on some phones, so it always has the 55 % scrim underneath.
  • Reminders that never fail. Exact-alarm permission, battery saver and notification channel state all need the phone-health row shown on this board, wired to real checks.

Keeping the look inside Glance limits

  • Colour: Material You dynamic colour. GlanceTheme reads the wallpaper’s tonal palette; there are no fixed brand colours in widgets. The surfaces on this board are the exact roles Glance exposes (surface, primary, secondary container and so on).
  • Fixed marks: the Ane rose and Abi teal person marks and the burst seal are drawables, so they stay the same on every wallpaper.
  • Shape: rounded corners at about 24–28 dp, as Android 12+ widgets use.
  • Layout: the giant widget is one scrolling column of sections. The quick-action bar sits outside the scrolling list and does not scroll.
  • Not used: blur, glass, arbitrary shadows, custom fonts. Wavy progress is shown with a plain bar and the numeral beside it.
  • Discreet mode swaps each section’s content for its name and a count, so it is cheap to render and safe to show.

Accessibility

  • Targets: at least 48 dp. Small visuals (the +1 chip, the tick circles) get an invisible hit area of 48 dp.
  • Text: body 14 px or more, nothing under 11 px. Layouts use wrapping rows and flexible heights so larger system font sizes don’t clip.
  • Colour is never alone: Ane and Abi also carry a shape (cookie and clover) and a name. Overdue has a label as well as red. Done has a tick as well as a colour.
  • Motion: reduced motion turns springs into 100 ms fades and stops loops. Haptics stay on.
  • Screen readers: every icon-only button is labelled; the map and the handwriting have text equivalents (“Abi · At Home since 6:42 PM”, the written words).
