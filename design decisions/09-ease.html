Direction 09 of 09

Ease

Quiet, spacious and familiar, in a tint of your own.

  • Calm
  • Spacious
  • Familiar
  • Soft
  • Clear

Concept

Ease borrows the unhurried clarity of Apple’s own apps (large titles, grouped lists, one tint colour and a lot of air) and gives each of you a colour of your own: Blush for the pink phone, Sky for the blue one. Each screen has one thing to say and everything else steps back. A private app for two doesn’t need to shout.

How it feels

Springs and soft fades, never bounce. A light tap you can feel on done, +1 and send. A love note settles onto the screen and rests. Your phone is tinted in your colour, and Abi always shows up in theirs.

Built on Apple’s eight design principles

Apple’s design principles are tools for weighing priorities. This is how each one shows up in Ease.

01

Purpose

Make something meaningful.

One job per screen. Today opens with the love note and Abi; everything else is in reach but quiet.

02

Agency

Let people do things their own way.

Tap the circle, +1 or Snooze right where an item sits. Every destructive action has Undo. Eleven destinations, none more than one tap away.

03

Responsibility

Act in people’s best interest.

A calm lock cue where only the two of you can see, discreet mode for widgets, and location pausing one tap away.

04

Familiarity

Build on what people know.

Large titles, grouped lists, a floating tab bar, sheets and the tap-the-circle checklist: patterns people already know.

05

Flexibility

Adapt to diverse contexts and needs.

Light and dark in both tints, 48 dp targets, layouts that survive larger text, and colour is never the only signal.

06

Simplicity

Be clear and direct.

One tint per phone, 20 px margins, 36 px between sections, no shadows on cards, short labels. Every element earns its place.

07

Craft

Care about every detail.

Contrast measured from the tokens, optical type sizes, one radius scale, tabular numerals, a real Android status bar and gesture pill.

08

Delight

Make it human.

A love note settles in softly, done fills a check, +1 rolls up, with a light tap you can feel. Warmth comes from your photos and your colours, not decoration.

On the home screen

The widgets use solid fills, rounded corners and the phone’s own font, so they stay simple to build.

Giant widgetOne scrolling column: love note, Abi, today’s reminders, then the scratchpad in full. The quick-action bar stays put at the bottom. Fixed brand tint (not Material You), so each phone keeps its own colour.

Small widgetsThe giant widget at medium size, plus Counter, Countdown, Love note, List, New note and Partner on a four-column grid.

Quick noteTap New note and a light editor opens over the dimmed home screen with the keyboard ready. Save returns straight home.

In the app

Large titles, grouped lists and a floating tab bar. Sections are 36 px apart; nothing touches.

TodayThe whole dashboard as one scroll: the love note first, then Abi, your day, mood, scratchpad, what’s coming up, photos, On this day, and the rest of the app in Browse.

ChatMessages you send take your tint; Abi’s stay neutral. Everything in the sample is here: photos, quote reply and reaction, voice note, handwriting, love note, sticker, seen and typing. No call buttons.

PhotosA calm grid with a small dot for who added each photo. Library, All, Albums, Favorites, Map and On this day scroll; the Vault keeps its own lock.

RemindersTap the circle to finish, Snooze when it isn’t the time. Overdue is the only red. The goal counter fills as you go.

NotesPlain text and handwriting in one note. Abi’s cursor and name appear live. No formatting bar, no checkboxes.

UsDays together as the one big number, then countdowns, this week’s plans, lists, wishlists (with a lock for secret ideas) and the yearly recap.

MapA quiet stylised map. Abi’s pin and accuracy circle, your pin on the move, and a sheet with pause options and saved places.

AlarmFull-screen over the lock screen. Done is the one big button; Snooze defaults to 10 minutes.

Dark mode

Not an inversion. Surfaces step up in lightness instead of casting shadows, and each tint is re-picked to stay readable on black.

Notes for the build

Mostly stock layout with a custom floating tab bar, so there is little to draw by hand beyond the handwriting canvas and the map.

Stock vs custom in Flutter

  • Stock, restyled: ListView with grouped cards, a large collapsing title (SliverAppBar.large), switches, text fields, modal bottom sheets, snackbars, segmented buttons.
  • Custom: the floating tab capsule, the love-note card and its arrival, the goal bar and rolling number, the voice waveform painter, the handwriting canvas (perfect_freehand), the SVG map style, the Wrapped gradient.
  • Colours come from one ThemeExtension with the same names as the CSS variables. The partner tint is just the other version’s tint.

Risky to build

  • Blur: only a few static surfaces use it (tab bar, chat header and composer, handwriting toolbar, map pills). With Reduce transparency or a slow GPU they fall back to the opaque surface colour.
  • Inter optical sizes: the google_fonts package fetches static weights; the opsz axis needs the variable TTF bundled. Without it, large titles lose a little tightness.
  • Floating tab bar over scrolling content: every scroll view needs bottom padding for the bar (about 124 px with the gesture inset).
  • Tall screens (Today, Us) are shown as one long capture; they scroll in the app.

Widgets within Glance limits

  • Solid surface, 28 dp corners, no blur, no shadow, the phone’s system font (Roboto here). Everything on the widget is a plain row, a progress bar, a round button or a bitmap.
  • Fixed brand tint, not Material You. Each phone keeps its own pink or blue so the home screen matches the app. Light and dark follow the system.
  • Handwriting on the scratchpad and love notes is a pre-rendered bitmap. The quick-action bar sits outside the scrolling LazyColumn.
  • Discreet mode swaps rows for section names and counts.

Accessibility

  • Contrast is computed from the tokens in the colour tables above; every text pair clears 4.5:1 in both tints, light and dark. label-3 is never used for text.
  • Visual sizes are 34–44 dp but every control has a 48 dp hit area (pad the tap region, not the pill).
  • Layouts use minimum heights and wrap, so larger system text pushes content down instead of clipping. Widgets size in sp.
  • Colour is never the only signal: Abi is also an avatar with initials and a name, overdue says “Overdue”, done is a filled check.
  • Reduced motion swaps springs for 150 ms fades and removes the arrival slide. Haptics follow the system setting.

To confirm with the owners

  • Which person is pink and which is blue. In the app it’s one choice at setup, sent to the other phone with the profile.
  • Today and Us are long single captures rather than 915 px frames, because the airy spacing doesn’t fit the usual height.
  • Inter instead of Apple’s SF, since SF can’t ship on Android.
