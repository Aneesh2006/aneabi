The concept

A couple’s app is full of in-jokes, stickers and tiny rituals, so it should feel like a sticker sheet, not a bank. Every tappable thing is a sticker: a thick ink outline, a flat colour and a hard offset shadow. It is obvious what to press, and a love note lands on your home screen like something Abi actually stuck there. Ane is always cobalt, Abi is always tomato, and sunshine yellow means “do this”.

How it feels

Pressed buttons drop into their shadow (4 px, 90 ms) with a light tick haptic. A love note slaps on with a springy overshoot and a double thunk. +1 pops a droplet into the counter; Done stamps a tick and strikes the line. Everything bounces fast and settles, and with reduced motion it becomes a plain fade.

In the app

The Home dashboard as one long scroll, then chat, gallery, reminders, the note editor, the “us” hub, the map and the full-screen alarm. Same stickers on every screen: outline, flat fill, hard shadow.

03

Dark mode

The system is translated, not inverted: outlines and hard shadows turn cream, large fills deepen (the love note goes plum, the scratchpad goes ochre) while small stickers keep their saturated colour so they still pop like stickers on a dark laptop.

Reply

The button translates by its own shadow offset (4 px, 4 px) while the shadow shrinks to 0, in 90 ms. Release springs back with --ease-snap. Small buttons use 2 px.

Haptics: press = selection tick · love note arrives = two heavy taps 90 ms apart (“thunk-thunk”) · done and +1 = medium impact. Reduced motion: every move below becomes a 120 ms fade; shadows and states stay.

0 ms: scale 1.6, tilt −9°, transparent. 220 ms: lands at 0.94 (haptic thunk-thunk). 420 ms: settles at 1.0 and keeps a −2° tilt until you dismiss it. On the home screen widget it simply appears (widgets cannot animate).

2 · +1 on a counter

The +1 button presses in (90 ms). The next empty cell drops in from 26 px above, overshoots by about 12 % and settles (240 ms) while the numeral rolls from 5 to 6. Reaching 8/8 stamps a streak sticker.

The check box stamps in: from 2.2× and −14° down to 0.92×, its shadow collapsing from 12 px to 0, then settling. A 3 px line strikes the title left to right (280 ms). Medium haptic. Undo lives in the snackbar.

Notes for the build

What is stock, what is custom, what is risky, and how the widgets keep the look inside Glance.

Stock in Flutter

  • Scaffold, NavigationBar, ListView, Switch, TextField, SnackBar: stock widgets, restyled through ThemeData from the colour, radius and type tokens.
  • Fonts: google_fonts for Archivo Black and Space Grotesk, plus Noto Color Emoji. Widgets use the phone’s own Roboto.
  • Icons: one set only, Tabler, via a Tabler icon package for Flutter. Names map one to one with the board.
  • Photos and lists: GridView.builder tiles with a 2 px border and a small person-colour mark.

Custom, built once

  • StickerBox: one widget that draws fill, 2 px outline and the 4 px hard shadow. Every card, chip, button and bubble is a StickerBox with a fill token.
  • Press behaviour: a pressed StickerBox translates by its shadow offset and drops the shadow, 90 ms, --ease-snap.
  • Handwriting: CustomPainter with round-cap strokes, stored as point lists and rendered to a bitmap for the widget.
  • Stickers, map, waveform: inline SVG on the board; build them as flutter_svg assets or painters. The map is drawn, with no tiles.
  • Tilt (±3°): Transform.rotate on tags, badges and the love-note label only.

Risky or fiddly to build

  • Hard shadows on scrolling lists: many offset shadows can cost frames. Use a plain DecoratedBox with a solid second box, never BoxShadow with blur, and keep repaint boundaries per row.
  • Love-note die-cut sticker: a white outline around an irregular shape needs a pre-rendered or path-stroked asset.
  • Alarm screen: native Compose over the lock screen; the sunburst is a vector drawable, not an animation loop, so it also works on low-end phones.
  • Quick-note scrim: window blur exists on some phones only; the dark scrim shown here is the design and the fallback.
  • Reduced motion: bounce and overshoot become a 120 ms fade; shadows and states stay the same.

How the widgets stay inside Glance

  • Look: a layered drawable: ink rectangle underneath, fill with 2 dp stroke inset 4 dp right and bottom on top. No blur, no glass, no arbitrary shadows.
  • Type: the system font only, Roboto Black for headings, Roboto Medium for rows. No custom fonts.
  • Colour: fixed brand colours with a day and a night resource set; Material You is not used.
  • Layout: the giant widget is a scrolling LazyColumn of four sections; the quick-action bar sits in a fixed row below it and never scrolls.
  • Images: handwriting, the cooking-dinner avatar and the Goa cover are bitmaps; icons are vector drawables.
  • Discreet mode: a second layout with names and counts only, chosen by a stored setting.

Accessibility

  • Contrast: text pairs are checked in the Colour section: all at 4.5:1 or better in both themes. Tomato and sunshine are never text colours on paper.
  • Colour is never alone: every person colour travels with “An” or “Ab”, a name or an icon. Overdue is red with a warning icon and the word.
  • Touch targets: 48 dp minimum. Small buttons are drawn 40 dp tall with a 48 dp hit area; widget buttons carry the same padding.
  • Type size: body text 14 sp or more, nothing under 11, and layouts wrap rather than clip at larger system text.
  • Outlines help: the 2 px ink outline gives every control a clear boundary, which suits low vision and bright sunlight.
  • Screen readers: assignee dots, mood faces, streak flames and the sticker tilt are decorative; each control has a text label for TalkBack.
